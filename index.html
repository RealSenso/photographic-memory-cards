1. The Translation (In Plain Words)
The feasible set Ω is the allowed playground. The active set 𝒜(x) represents the constraints whose boundary you are physically leaning against right now. Inactive constraints (g_j(x) < 0) have room to spare and don't restrict immediate local steps.
2. Formal Math
$$\Omega = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^n : h_i(x) = 0, g_j(x) \le 0\}$$ $$\mathcal{A}(x) = \{j : g_j(x) = 0\}$$
3. Personal Mental Model
"Standing in the corner of a room: only the two walls you are touching prevent you from walking through them. The far wall across the room has zero influence on your immediate next step."
⚠️ Exam Trap: Never plug inactive constraints into your local derivative checks. Since g_j(x) < 0 is continuous, it remains valid in an open neighborhood!
